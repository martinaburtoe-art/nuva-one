# Nüva One — Design Context

## Product
Premium SaaS workspace for Chilean pymes and medium-to-large businesses. The authenticated product supports daily business operations: sales, point of sale, inventory, expenses, cash, finance, reporting, customer workflows and business intelligence.

## Users and jobs
Owners, administrators and operational staff need to understand the state of the business, identify the next important action and complete workflows with confidence. The interface must support frequent daily use, dense operational information and fast task completion on desktop and mobile.

## Brand personality
Precise. Assured. Distinctive. Calm, not sterile; premium, not ornamental; intelligent, not futuristic for its own sake.

## Aesthetic direction
- Authenticated workspace uses warm pearl/light surfaces, clean whites and warm grays.
- Dark navigation rail anchors the product shell.
- Nüva indigo #6366F1 is the action/focus color.
- Muted gold #E6C687 and chart gold #B99A5D are restrained brand accents.
- Quiet borders, controlled radii, purposeful negative space and clear information hierarchy.
- Design should feel like a mature business instrument, not a template or AI-generated dashboard.

## Design principles
1. **Clarity before decoration.** Every surface has a clear focal point and obvious next action.
2. **Cohesion across modules.** Reuse the authenticated shell, semantic tokens, type scale, control heights and component states.
3. **Operational density with legibility.** Prioritize decision-useful information; use tabular numerals for money and metrics.
4. **Mobile is a designed surface.** Respect safe areas, dynamic viewport, touch targets, keyboard, sheets, filters and chart readability.
5. **Motion communicates state.** Use short, meaningful transitions; respect reduced motion; no infinite shimmer or decorative ambient effects.
6. **Honest product states.** Loading, empty, error, stale and populated data must be distinct and truthful.

## Anti-references
Generic purple/blue gradients, gratuitous glassmorphism, oversized decorative headings in operational screens, repeated equal-card grids, arbitrary accent colors, excessive rounding, fake metrics, placeholder actions and motion that competes with work.

## Technical constraints
React + TypeScript + Vite + TanStack Router + Tailwind. Preserve current architecture and existing component primitives. Do not initialize or wholesale-replace shadcn/ui. Avoid new dependencies unless they solve a demonstrated functional need. Public marketing/auth/checkout surfaces are outside the authenticated-workspace design scope unless explicitly requested.

## Responsive validation targets
320, 360, 390, 430 px; tablet; desktop. Check horizontal overflow, clipped controls, sticky actions, safe-area insets, keyboard overlap, touch targets, reduced motion and chart/filter readability.
