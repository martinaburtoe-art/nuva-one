# Nüva One — Video generation batch

## Production rule
Generate all chapter masters as one coherent film universe. Do not generate 14 unrelated SaaS clips.

### Character continuity
Use the supplied character sheet as the recurring protagonist reference: adult woman, short dark wavy hair, genuine smile, neutral linen apron, olive/neutral clothing, natural movement.

### Location continuity
Use the supplied business image as the spatial reference: warm natural light, timber frames, pale wood shelving/counter, premium neighborhood shop, realistic products, restrained natural palette.

### Cinematic grammar
- photorealistic live-action;
- 16:9 master;
- 8 s target;
- 24 fps look;
- natural camera motion;
- no floating cubes;
- no generic futuristic holograms;
- no random UI pasted over faces/hands;
- preserve physical continuity between shots;
- start/end frames must allow crossfade or match-cut;
- camera movement should feel like a continuous dolly, push, pan, reveal or macro transition;
- typography is added by the web layer, not generated inside the video.

## Chapter prompts

### 01 Hero
Open the shop at early morning. The same protagonist enters and prepares the counter. Slow forward dolly from the doorway into the business. End near the counter with a clear forward visual direction for Sales.

### 02 Sales
Continue from the counter. A customer approaches; the protagonist naturally completes a sale. Camera follows the exchange, then settles on the product/payment moment. End with a subtle movement toward the customer for Customers.

### 03 Customers
Follow the same customer relationship rather than showing a CRM dashboard. Warm human interaction, recognition, repeat visit. Camera moves from face to wider shop context. End by drifting toward shelving for Inventory.

### 04 Inventory
Move from the customer area into shelving. Protagonist checks product availability naturally. Slow lateral dolly through stocked shelves, ending on one product that needs attention. Prepare transition to Scanner.

### 05 Scanner
Use the supplied real Scanner video when available. Scrub this master by scroll. Preserve the physical phone/product action and use it as the bridge from real-world inventory to digital control.

### 06 Purchases
Continue from the scanned product concept. Protagonist notices a low-stock item and prepares a purchase/restock decision. Camera moves from shelf detail to a broader operational view.

### 07 Cash
Follow the business flow from purchase/order to payment/cash. Physical transaction, receipt, drawer or payment terminal. Calm controlled camera movement, ending with a money/detail close-up.

### 08 Shipping
Move from order preparation to packing and dispatch. Track a parcel from counter to outgoing area. Camera exits the workspace with the package, creating spatial expansion.

### 09 Finance
Pull back into a calm wider view of the shop. The protagonist pauses and understands the business. Use visual breathing room; avoid dashboard overload. End with a deliberate forward camera move.

### 10 Nüva Score
Translate the idea of business health into visual confidence: protagonist observes a concise digital summary on a laptop/phone, then looks back at the shop. UI is subtle and added by the web layer where possible.

### 11 Automations
Show the business continuing smoothly while routine actions happen with less manual intervention. Camera glides through small operational moments rather than depicting magical AI effects.

### 12 Nüva Studio
Quiet desk moment. Protagonist asks a question / reviews information and immediately understands the next action. Camera moves from human expression to screen detail, then back out.

### 13 Connections
Reveal the complete ecosystem: shop, phone, laptop, orders, stock and dispatch as one coherent operation. Large pull-back / orbit feeling. Prepare return to opening composition.

### 14 Final
Return visually to the Hero environment, now calmer and more organized. Same character, same shop, same light family. Slow final pull-back. Leave clean negative space for Nüva One closing typography.
