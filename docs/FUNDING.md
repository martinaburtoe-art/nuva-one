Last verification: 2026-08-25.

## Sercotec Capital Semilla Emprende 2026

Official source: https://www.sercotec.cl/programas/capital-semilla-emprende/

The program is designed to support new businesses through formalization and startup. The official program page states a subsidy of $3,500,000, with $200,000–$500,000 for management actions and $3,000,000–$3,300,000 for eligible investments/capital of work, subject to the applicable bases.

Important eligibility signal: the program page states applicants must be adults without first-category commencement of activities before the relevant call and must meet the regional focus and other requirements.

## Maule 2026 verification

Sercotec's current call calendar lists a 2026 Capital Semilla Modo Empleo call for Región del Maule with a closing date shown as 08-09-2026 at 15:00. This is a specific call and must not be conflated with the general Capital Semilla Emprende program.

## Applicant-status rule

Before submitting any application, verify against the exact call bases:

- applicant type;
- age/category requirements;
- first-category activity status;
- existing company participation;
- debts and compliance;
- territorial eligibility;
- co-financing;
- eligible expenses;
- application window;
- required attachments.

## Corfo / Start-Up Chile / other instruments

Do not mark an instrument as open from historical pages. Each instrument requires a fresh official-source verification immediately before submission.

## Evidence policy

No traction, customers, pilots, revenue, interviews or market validation may be inserted into a funding application without primary evidence.

Unknown values must remain `TBD`, `DATA REQUIRED` or `EXTERNAL EVIDENCE REQUIRED`.
