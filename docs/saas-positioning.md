# Nüva One — SaaS Positioning

## Product promise

**Nüva One no solo muestra lo que pasó. Te ayuda a entender qué está pasando y qué hacer después.**

## Product loop

1. **Operar** — ventas, inventario, compras, cotizaciones, clientes y finanzas.
2. **Entender** — Nüva Score, indicadores y Explícame mi negocio.
3. **Decidir** — Nüva IA, recomendaciones y prioridades.
4. **Anticipar** — alertas, tendencias y señales de riesgo/oportunidad.

## Commercial narrative

Landing → Showcase → Demo → Conversión → Onboarding → Activación → Insight → Decisión.

The demo is intentionally a partial product preview. It must never imply that Nüva One is only the modules shown there.

## Differentiation

Nüva One should compete primarily on **business understanding and decision support**, not on having the largest number of ERP modules.

### Core message

> **Tu negocio genera datos todos los días. Nüva los convierte en decisiones.**

### Activation moment

Once a customer has enough real data, the dashboard should surface a concise insight such as:

> **Nüva encontró algo que deberías saber.**

The insight must identify the signal, explain why it matters, and offer a relevant next action.

## Product principles

- Preserve existing functionality while improving the experience around it.
- Never replace security, RLS, authentication, billing, or existing business logic for visual improvements.
- Prefer contextual recommendations over generic dashboards.
- Keep claims proportional to what the product actually implements.
- Treat the demo as a safe, fictional environment; never mix demo data with production business data.
